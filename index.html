Where the authentic tradition of Swiss Engadiner pastry meets the quiet art of baking, right here in West Java.

Rooted in the traditions of the Swiss Alps, the Engadiner Nusstorte is a legendary pastry from the Engadin valley of Switzerland. It features a delicate, buttery shortcrust enveloping a rich, unique filling of roasted nuts—walnut, almond, and cashew—bound together by a warm, slow-cooked cinnamon caramel. At LiebeFeld's, we bring this authentic heritage to Indonesia, baking strictly by weekly pre-order to ensure every tart is crafted with patience, premium ingredients, and the unhurried devotion that true Swiss pastry demands.
